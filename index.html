Support & FAQ

How much does Glovebox cost?

Glovebox is free to download and use for tracking your cars. A one-time Glovebox Pro unlock adds AI document extraction and build sheet export — no subscription.

Where is my data stored?

On your device, and synced through your personal iCloud account. There is no Glovebox server and no account to create. You can also export an encrypted backup to any cloud you choose.

Does the AI send my documents anywhere?

No. Document reading runs entirely on-device using Apple's on-device models. Your receipts and titles never leave your phone.

What do I need to run it?

An iPhone on a recent version of iOS. AI extraction uses Apple Intelligence and requires a supported device; everything else works everywhere.

Email support@gloveboxcars.com and we'll get back to you.
