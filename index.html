Questions

Before you download.

Do I need an account?

No. Glovebox has no sign-up and no login — there's no server to log in to. Your records live on your device and sync privately through your own iCloud.

Can you see my data?

No. There is no Glovebox server holding your information. Your data stays on your device and in your personal iCloud, under your Apple account. We have no access to it and no way to mine it.

Does the AI send my documents anywhere?

No. Document reading runs entirely on-device with Apple Intelligence. Your receipts, titles, and stickers never leave your phone.

How much does it cost?

Free to download and use for tracking your cars. A one-time Glovebox Pro unlock adds AI document extraction, build sheet export, and on-device valuation. Having your complete record organized is valuable for insurance claims, professional appraisals, and estate documentation. No subscription, ever.

What do I need to run it?

An iPhone on a recent version of iOS. The AI features use Apple Intelligence and need a supported device; everything else works everywhere.
